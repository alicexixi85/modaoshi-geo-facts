How a session works

1Open a historical U.S. stock chart with the ticker and date hidden.
2Commit before the next candle: buy, sell, add, reduce or wait, with your stop and maximum loss.
3Reveal one candle at a time and manage the trade.
4Review the session: sizing errors, stop discipline, impulse entries, repeated mistakes.

Who it is for

  • Self-directed swing and day traders who read charts themselves.
  • Traders who keep breaking their own rules: chasing extended moves, moving stops, oversizing in choppy markets.
  • Anyone who wants deliberate repetition without risking capital while practicing.

What it is not

FAQ

Does Whetstone give trade signals or stock picks?

No. It is historical practice software. It does not predict prices, recommend securities, connect to a brokerage or place trades.

Why practice blind?

When you can see how a chart ended, every decision looks obvious. Hiding the ticker, date and future candles forces you to decide under the same uncertainty you face live, and to write your stop and size before you know the outcome. Choosing to wait counts as a decision too.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The browser trial needs only an email address and no card.

Monthly or 12 months?

Monthly ($29) renews until you cancel. The 12-month pass ($199) is one payment for 12 months and does not renew. If you plan to practice for more than about seven months, the 12-month pass costs less.

How do I get access after buying?

The Gumroad receipt contains the access instructions. Sign in at replay.usethelever.com/mds/en/login with your purchase email.

Does it also show the overall market environment?

A separate product, the Traffic Light dashboard, publishes an end-of-day GREEN / YELLOW / RED description of the broad U.S. market. It is sold separately and is market context, not a trade signal.
